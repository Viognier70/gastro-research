    TRIAD — The Epistemological Model

    Aristotelian forms of knowledge and the Double Grip

    The TRIAD model applies Aristotle's three forms of knowledge to culinary and hospitality practice — forming a developmental hierarchy toward professional mastery. Every article in this library is translated into all three levels for your profession.

    1. When it applies — and when it doesn't
    The limits: context, exceptions, and where your judgement takes over
    2. What to do with it
    The applied move — technique, timing, workflow
    3. What the research found
    The finding, the evidence behind it, and how strong it is
    These three come from a model of professional knowledge. → How TRIAD reads a paper
