TRIAD — The Epistemological Model

Aristoteliska kunskapsformer och det dubbla greppet

The TRIAD model maps Aristotle's three forms of knowledge onto culinary and hospitality practice. They are not three equal alternatives — they form a developmental hierarchy. Every professional begins with Episteme, trains through Techne, and aspires toward Phronesis. Each research article in this library is translated into all three levels for your profession.

1. Sinnlig realisering — Phronesis · φρόνησις
Det situerade omdömet — där kunskap blir erfaren sanning
Phronesis is the apex and the integration principle. It is not just a third form of knowledge — it gives meaning to the other two. The sommelier with Phronesis moves fluidly between "What is in the glass?" (analytical) and "What does this evoke?" (analogical). This is the Double Grip in action: aesthetic and ethical judgment in a concrete, unrepeatable moment.
Uttrycksformer: Analogier, metaforer, narrativ · Kroppslig och temporal perception (attack, mitt, avslut) · Estetisk och etisk bedömning
Nyckelfråga: "What does this evoke?" — den analogiska frågan
2. Praktisk orkestrering — Techne · τέχνη
Det utförande kunnandet — där kunskap blir handling
Techne is craft knowledge — the skill that lives in the body and hands. It cannot be learned from textbooks alone. It requires deliberate practice, repetition and feedback. Techne mediates between Episteme and Phronesis: it is the trained capacity that makes analytical knowledge actionable and experiential wisdom repeatable.
Uttrycksformer: Servering, temperatur, glasval · Vinifiering, blending, logistik · Sensoriska protokoll och träningsmetoder
Nyckelfråga: "How do I do this?" — den procedurella frågan
3. Symbolisk inramning — Episteme · ἐπιστήμη
Det analytiska vetandet — där kunskap blir begrepp och struktur
Episteme is the foundation — universal, provable, teachable knowledge. It isolates and describes: flavour chemistry, sensory physiology, hospitality theory. In research terms, this is the IMRaD structure, the proven principle, the classification system. Every professional begins here, but mastery cannot end here.
Uttrycksformer: Syranivå, tanninstruktur, aromkategorier · QDA, flash profiling · Klassificeringar och typiciteter
Nyckelfråga: "What is in the glass?" — den analytiska frågan
The Double Grip — Det dubbla greppet
The movement between Episteme and Phronesis is not linear — it is a trained oscillation. "What is in the glass?" anchors you in analysis. "What does this evoke?" opens you to experience. Techne is the craft that makes this movement possible. The professional who has mastered the Double Grip does not choose between science and intuition — they move between them, fluidly, in every situation.

Modellens syfte: Att utveckla förmågan att växla och integrera mellan analytiska, praktiska och intuitiva kunskapsformer. Triaden tränas genom reflektion, dialog, erfarenhet och medveten praktik — det dubbla greppet i handling.
Crichton-Fock & Spence (2024) · Herdenstam (2011, 2024) · Based on Aristotle (trans. 2000)
